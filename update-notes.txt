Video orientation normalization: three independently calibrated phones exposed that QuietLink was mixing Camera2 sensor-relative rotation, Camera2/SurfaceTexture preview transforms, optional camera rotate-and-crop, H.264 rotation metadata, and TextureView presentation as though they were the same angle. v0.3.62 adds a capability-negotiated H.264 orientation protocol (ROT_CW1) with one wire meaning: clockwise rotation to display raw sensor-oriented H.264 pixels. Compatible peers opt out of Camera2 rotate-and-crop AUTO where supported, attach the canonical angle to H.264 access units, apply it directly only to decoded remote video, and leave local Camera2 TextureView sensor normalization alone except for display rotation + front-camera mirroring. Saved manual profiles remain stored and are automatically bypassed only while normalized mode is active; Developer Video Tune can force the legacy profile pipeline immediately if needed. Portrait-only app orientation remains enabled.
