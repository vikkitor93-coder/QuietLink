Video calibration clarity: compact tuning is now split by numbered windows. Window 1 is the large incoming video; Window 2 is the mini/self-camera preview. A Window 1/2 dropdown selects which window is being tuned, and matching number badges appear on the live video. Window 1 has independent incoming rotation/aspect controls; Window 2 has mini-preview rotation/aspect/mirror plus clearly separated sender-camera controls. Aspect options now include true portrait reciprocals: 9:16, 3:4, 2:3 and 4:5, plus 16:9, 4:3, 3:2, 5:4, 1:1, Auto and Stretch. Explicit ratios are treated as final visible width:height and are no longer silently flipped by quarter-turn rotation.
