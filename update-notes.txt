Reliability repair: diagnostic chat transfer no longer pushes the full raw log as hundreds of unpaced control frames or forces the entire call into recovery when the optional transfer fails. Logs are now gzip-compressed first, sent in bounded encrypted chunks with a visible send/receive progress bar, and reconstructed only after size + SHA-256 validation. Saved Video/Baby Inline/Fullscreen calibration profiles are embedded in the privacy-safe diagnostic .txt automatically. A privacy-safe uncaught-crash fingerprint is also retained for future diagnostics. Wi-Fi Direct CODE fallback is repaired: CODE again requests the optional Android nearby/location runtime permission when Wi-Fi is on without making it an internet prerequisite; P2P startup cleans stale groups/requests, retries BUSY/error states, retries service discovery, no longer permanently locks after a failed connect(), and no longer loses its one-shot fallback because a LAN socket attempt happened to be active at the 8-second mark.
